## What's new in SoVisu+ {SOVISUPLUS_VERSION}

This help describes SoVisu+ {SOVISUPLUS_VERSION}, the version shown at the top of every page. Each institution updates
SoVisu+ at its own pace: your instance may not be on this version yet.

Compared with version 1.3, the 1.6 series (versions 1.6.0 to 1.6.4, September and October 2026) brings:

- **A more tolerant search**, for typos and spelling variants.
- **Chart download**, for the Concepts and collaborations chart: see [Export a chart](/en/guides/visualiser/exporter-un-graphique/).
- **Edit account**, available from the dashboard and the Structures page, for authorized people.
- **Deposit in HAL** from a publication record, if your institution has configured it: see
  [Deposit in HAL](/en/guides/hal/deposer-dans-hal/).
- **An IdRef can no longer be deleted** by its holder: see [Identifiers](/en/donnees/identifiants/).

Features announced but not available yet are listed in [Upcoming features](/en/donnees/fonctions-a-venir/). This help has
been online since November 2026.
