## Log in

Log in with your institution's account, from the SoVisu+ home page.

Login only succeeds if your account matches a person known to SoVisu+, and therefore present in the directory sent by
your institution. If it is refused, see [Support](/en/guides/support/).

## What you can do

Everyone can **view**: dashboard, publications and structures, for yourself and for others. **Editing** requires a role,
which your institution may or may not have given you.

| Profile | You can also | Where to start |
|---|---|---|
| **Account without a role** | Nothing more than viewing | [Read my profile](/en/guides/mon-profil/lire-mon-profil/), [Read the dashboard](/en/guides/visualiser/lire-le-tableau-de-bord/) |
| **Researcher on their own record** | Correct your publications, merge them, restart their collection | [Make my publications reliable](/en/tutoriels/fiabiliser-mes-publications/) |
| **Contact with a scope** | Do the same for a unit or an institution | [Clean up your laboratory's corpus](/en/tutoriels/referent-labo-nettoyer-le-corpus/) |
| **Administrator** | Everything, on all records | [Roles and rights](/en/donnees/roles-et-droits/) |

SoVisu+ does not display your profile explicitly, and has no rights management screen. A simple clue: if the **Update all
publications** button is grayed out on your own publications, you do not have the corresponding role.

## Your first visit

1. Open **See my account** and check **My affiliations** and **My identifiers**.
2. Open **Publications**: the page is titled **My publications** and lists what SoVisu+ knows about you.
3. If a publication is missing, or is not yours, see
   [A publication is missing](/en/guides/corriger/publication-manquante/) or
   [A publication is wrongly attributed](/en/guides/corriger/publication-attribuee-a-tort/).
4. Open **Dashboard** to see these publications per year and your collaborations.

## Known limits

- Any logged-in account can view most of the data of other perspectives: do not assume that what you see here is
  restricted to your laboratory.
- A person missing from the directory sent to SoVisu+ has no account, even if they appear among the co-authors of a
  publication.
