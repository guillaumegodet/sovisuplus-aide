- **Administrator**: account with full rights on the instance.
- **Affiliation**: structure indicated for a contributor on a publication. Not to be confused with membership of a
  structure, which comes from the directory.
- **Align**: link a contributor of a publication to their HAL profile.
- **Assistant**: conversational agent built into SoVisu+, if your institution has enabled it.
- **Authenticated identifier**: identifier whose holder has proved ownership by logging in to the service, for example
  ORCID or HAL.
- **Contribution role**: a contributor's function on a publication: author, editor, thesis advisor, etc.
- **Contributor**: person linked to a publication, with one or more contribution roles.
- **Directory**: list of people and structures sent by your institution to SoVisu+.
- **Dissociate**: remove from a publication a source reference that does not belong to it. Not available yet, see
  [Upcoming features](/en/donnees/fonctions-a-venir/).
- **eppn**: login identifier of a person in their federation of institutions.
- **HAL**: French multidisciplinary open archive.
- **HAL collection**: set of HAL deposits attached to a structure.
- **HAL deposit**: publication deposited in the HAL open archive, with or without a file.
- **Harvesting**: collection of references from the sources. In the interface: **Update all publications**.
- **IdHAL**: identifier of an author in HAL.
- **IdRef**: authority reference of the French academic libraries network.
- **Membership**: link between a person and a structure, sent by the directory.
- **Merge**: group several publications that describe the same work.
- **Open Access**: possibility of reading a publication freely. See [Open access and HAL](/en/donnees/acces-ouvert-et-hal/).
- **OpenAlex**: open catalog of scientific publications.
- **ORCID**: free international identifier for a researcher.
- **Perspective**: person or structure whose data you are looking at.
- **Publication (or document)**: record built from one or more source references.
- **Role**: set of rights given to an account. See [Roles and rights](/en/donnees/roles-et-droits/).
- **ScanR**: research and innovation search engine of the French ministry.
- **Scope**: what a role applies to: a person, a unit, an institution.
- **Scopus**: bibliographic database published by Elsevier.
- **Source reference**: version of a publication as described by a source, for example HAL or OpenAlex.
- **Structure**: research unit, team, institution or other entity of the structure tree.
- **Supervising institution**: institution on which a research structure depends.
- **Unpaywall**: service that lists open access versions of publications.
- **Wordstream**: the **Concepts and collaborations** chart of the dashboard.
