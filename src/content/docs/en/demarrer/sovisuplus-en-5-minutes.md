SoVisu+ brings together in one place the publications of a researcher or a research structure, the identifiers used
to find them, and visualizations to read them. Depending on your rights, you can also correct this information there.

:::note[Your institution may differ]
Some features depend on your institution's configuration and on your rights. See
[Instances and configuration](/en/donnees/instances-et-configuration/) and [Roles and rights](/en/donnees/roles-et-droits/).
:::

## Where the data comes from

SoVisu+ does not ask you to enter your publications. Its data has two origins:

- **people and structures** come from your institution's directory;
- **publications** are collected from HAL, ScanR, IdRef, OpenAlex and Scopus, using each person's identifiers.

Details are in [Sources and harvesting](/en/donnees/sources-et-moissonnage/).

## The key notion: the perspective

The **perspective** is the person or structure whose data you are looking at. When you log in, that is you. The search
field of the side bar, **Search**, lets you change it: a researcher, a research unit, a team, an institution. The Dashboard
and Publications pages are then displayed for this perspective.

## The menu pages

| Page | What you find there |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Publications per year and their open access rate, concepts and collaborations, the map of worldwide collaborations |
| **Publications** | The list of publications of the perspective, their open access and HAL statuses, and the detailed record of each one |
| **Structures** | Research structures as a list, a hierarchical table or a tree, with their members |
| **Expertise** | Page still under construction, see [Upcoming features](/en/donnees/fonctions-a-venir/) |

At the bottom of the side bar, your name opens the account menu, and **See my account** leads to your identifiers and
affiliations. **Help** opens this help or a contact, depending on your institution.

## Three habits

1. **Check your identifiers**: a missing identifier loses publications, a wrong one adds publications that are not
   yours. See [Identifiers](/en/donnees/identifiants/).
2. **Before correcting, ask yourself where the error is**: in the directory, in the original source or in SoVisu+.
   See [Where to fix what](/en/guides/corriger/ou-corriger-quoi/).
3. **Read the limits**: each page of this help states them. For example, a structure's indicators cover the last
   24 months.
