Each institution installs and manages its own SoVisu+ instance. You should therefore contact its local team, often the
library or the research office, through your institution's usual channel.

:::note[Nantes Université]
Write to [guillaume.godet@univ-nantes.fr](mailto:guillaume.godet@univ-nantes.fr).
:::

## Before writing

Check whether the help answers your question:

- wrong data: [Where to fix what](/en/guides/corriger/ou-corriger-quoi/);
- a missing publication: [A publication is missing](/en/guides/corriger/publication-manquante/);
- a publication that is not yours: [A publication is wrongly attributed](/en/guides/corriger/publication-attribuee-a-tort/);
- a grayed-out or missing button: [Roles and rights](/en/donnees/roles-et-droits/);
- a "work in progress" page: [Upcoming features](/en/donnees/fonctions-a-venir/).

## What to include

| Request | What to include |
|---|---|
| **Login refused** | Your login identifier, the message displayed |
| **Right to obtain** | What you need to be able to do, and over which scope: your record, a unit, an institution |
| **Profile data** | The wrong data and the right value, with a source if possible |
| **Publication** | The DOI or link of the publication, and the person or structure concerned |
| **Malfunction** | What you were doing, what happened, the exact message, your browser |

:::caution
Do not send screenshots containing personal data through an unsecured channel: describe the problem and give the link to
the page.
:::

## Known limits

The SoVisu+ **Help** button may lead to this help or to a contact address, or be absent, depending on your institution. See
[Instances and configuration](/en/donnees/instances-et-configuration/).
