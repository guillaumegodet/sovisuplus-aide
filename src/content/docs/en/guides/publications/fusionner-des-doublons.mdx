Two records describe the same work, often because the sources have slightly different titles. If the checkboxes of the list
are inactive, you do not have this right: go through [Support](/en/guides/support/).

:::caution[No undo in the interface]
In version 1.6.4, **Dissociate** does not work: a wrong merge cannot be undone from SoVisu+. Check carefully before
confirming.
:::

## Steps

1. In **Publications**, find the records with the [search](/en/guides/publications/rechercher-filtrer/).
2. Check at least two publications, in the first column.
3. Click **Merge selected documents**.
4. In **Merge publications**, compare titles, dates and authors. **Detail** opens a record.
5. Leave checked only the publications to group.
6. Click **Confirm merge**, with the number of publications in parentheses.

## What happens next

The merge is not immediate: the record stays locked during processing, then the result appears.

## Known limits

- **A publication being updated** cannot be selected.
- **A researcher on their own record** can only merge publications they contributed to.
- **Merging changes nothing in the sources**: a duplicate in HAL must be dealt with in HAL.
- **Beware of versions**: an article and its thesis, or two editions, are not necessarily duplicates.
