Two columns sum up the situation of each publication. Details of the rules are in
[Open access and HAL](/en/donnees/acces-ouvert-et-hal/).

## Steps

1. Read **Open Access Status**: GOLD, DIAMOND, HYBRID, BRONZE, GREEN, OTHER, CLOSED or UNKNOWN.
2. Read **Status in HAL**: **In HAL** or **Out of HAL**.
3. Hover over the HAL badge to see details and actions.

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| **In HAL**, with type **Notice**, **File** or **Annex** | **See document on HAL** to check the deposit |
| **In HAL**, with a reference to the **affiliation** | Check the affiliation declared in HAL: the publication is not in the expected collection |
| **Publication unavailable in HAL** | Check your **IdHAL in My Account**; deposit if the publication really is not there |
| **IdHAL is missing** | Enter your IdHAL, or create one on the HAL website |

## Known limits

- **The badge depends on the perspective**: the same publication can be in one laboratory's collection and not another's.
- **Action buttons require rights**: the deposit button is not offered everywhere.
- **A wrong status is fixed at the source**: see [Where to fix what](/en/guides/corriger/ou-corriger-quoi/).
