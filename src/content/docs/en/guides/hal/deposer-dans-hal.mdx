The **Add in HAL** tab appears for a publication **with no HAL reference**. If it is absent, depositing is not open to you:
you can still deposit directly on the HAL website.

## Before you start

| You need | Where to do it |
|---|---|
| An **IdHAL** and a linked HAL account | [Check my identifiers](/en/guides/mon-profil/verifier-identifiants/) |
| A **publication date** | **Bibliographic information** |
| A **title** and an **abstract** in French and English | **Bibliographic information** |
| A **journal**, for an article | **Bibliographic information** |
| At least one **identified affiliation** | [Align an author with HAL](/en/guides/fiche-publication/aligner-un-auteur-hal/) |
| A keyword in French and one in English, for a thesis or HDR | [Manage keywords](/en/guides/fiche-publication/gerer-les-mots-cles/) |

## Steps

1. Complete the **Deposit metadata**. The title, abstract, date, journal and authors are taken from the other tabs.
2. Choose the **HAL domains**.
3. Add the **Main file (PDF)**, with its **License**, and any complementary files. Depending on the document type, **Notice
   only (no file)** is possible.
4. Click **Review**, read the **Deposit recap**, then **Confirm deposit**.

## What happens next

| Status | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Deposit in progress** | Wait |
| **Under moderation** | Wait: 1 to 5 working days |
| **Published on HAL** | [Update your publications](/en/guides/publications/mettre-a-jour-les-publications/) to see the HAL source |
| **Changes requested** | Apply them on the HAL website, then **Refresh status** |
| **Rejected** | See the moderators' message |
| **Submission failed** | Read the **Error message:** and try again |

## Known limits

- **You are responsible for the rights to the file**: check the publisher's policy before depositing.
- **An affiliation not recognized by HAL is not sent**: a message warns you.
- **Updating an existing deposit is not available**: see [Upcoming features](/en/donnees/fonctions-a-venir/).
