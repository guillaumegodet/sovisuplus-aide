## Steps

1. Open a structure, for example in the **Tree**.
2. Read the **Members** list: **Name**, **Position**, **Arrival**, **Departure**, **Publications**, **Identifiers**.
3. **Currently present**, on by default, limits the list to people present: turn it off to see former members.
4. **View this person's publications** opens their publications.
5. With the corresponding right, **Edit account** opens their profile.

## Known limits

- **The list comes from the directory**: a missing member or wrong date is fixed there. See [Where to fix what](/en/guides/corriger/ou-corriger-quoi/).
- **An institution's members** are its employees, whereas its publications go through its research units: the two figures do not cover the same people.
