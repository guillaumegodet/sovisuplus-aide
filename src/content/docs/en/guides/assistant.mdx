The **Assistant** answers in plain language questions about the research data known to the knowledge graph: a person, a
structure, their publications.

## Steps

1. Click the round button at the bottom right of the page.
2. Write your question in **Ask the assistant…**, then click **Send**.
3. Start another topic with **New conversation**. **Back to conversations** finds previous ones.

When you write "I" or "my publications", the assistant understands that this means you.

## Known limits

- **Check its answers.** It can be wrong, or answer from incomplete data.
- **It is not for corrections**: for wrong data, see [Where to fix what](/en/guides/corriger/ou-corriger-quoi/).
- **It can be slow or unavailable**: a message says so, and **Retry** sends the request again.
- **It does not search this help**: for a question about how SoVisu+ works, use the search at the top of this page.
