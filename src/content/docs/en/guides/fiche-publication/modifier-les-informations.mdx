Without an edit button, you do not have this right: go through [Support](/en/guides/support/).

## Steps

| To change | Click | Then |
|---|---|---|
| The title | **Edit title** | Type, **Add Language** if needed, **Save** |
| The abstract | **Edit abstract** | Same |
| The document type | **Modify** next to the type | **Select a type**, **Apply** |
| The date | **Modify** next to the date | Choose the date or **Remove date**, **Apply** |

## What happens next

The correction is not immediate. While it is processed, the record says: "This document is being updated and cannot
be edited right now." A message then offers **Update view**.

## Known limits

- **An empty field is not saved**, and at least one field must be filled in.
- **Available languages** depend on your institution.
- **Published in** and **Identifiers** cannot be edited here.
- **The source is not corrected**: the error remains in HAL or OpenAlex. See [Where to fix what](/en/guides/corriger/ou-corriger-quoi/).
