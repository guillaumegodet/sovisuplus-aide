The **Authors** tab lists the **Contributors** and their **Affiliations**. **Change authors**, in the bibliographic
information, also leads there.

## Steps

| To | Do |
|---|---|
| Add a person | **Add a contributor** or **Insert contributor here**, then **Search a profile in HAL** and **Add contributor** |
| Remove a person | **Remove contributor** |
| Change the order | **Ranking mode**, then drag, or **Move up** and **Move down** |
| Correct the role | **Roles**, then **Select roles** |
| Attach to the right profile | See [Align an author with HAL](/en/guides/fiche-publication/aligner-un-auteur-hal/) |

**Save** at the end: the banner "You have unsaved changes in this tab." stays displayed until you do. Leaving the tab asks for
confirmation.

## What happens next

The correction is not immediate: the record stays locked while it is processed.

## Known limits

- **"Default role - please check"** flags a role assigned by default: check it, as it can exclude the publication from some
  lists. See [Contributions and retained roles](/en/donnees/contributions-et-roles-retenus/).
- **Profile search queries HAL**: at least 2 characters are needed, and HAL may be temporarily unavailable.
- **The source is not corrected**: see [Where to fix what](/en/guides/corriger/ou-corriger-quoi/).
