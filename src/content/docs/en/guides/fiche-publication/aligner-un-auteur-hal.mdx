Aligning means telling SoVisu+ which HAL profile matches a contributor. Each contributor's label shows where they stand.

| Label | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Identified and aligned** | The contributor has a HAL identifier |
| **Identified** | They have an ORCID or IdRef, but no HAL identifier |
| **Not aligned** | They were added with no match in HAL |
| **Not identified** | No identifier |

## Steps: the profile

1. On the contributor, click **Suggest** to get **matches in HAL**.
2. In the right **HAL suggestion**, check the publications displayed, then click **Confirm**.
3. Otherwise, use **Change the identification** and **Search a profile in HAL**.

## Steps: affiliations

1. Look for **Missing HAL affiliation**. **Imported text:** shows what the source says.
2. Click **Suggest**, then **Align** on the right structure.
3. Otherwise, **Add HAL affiliation**, then **Search an organization in HAL**. Prefer a **PREFERRED** structure.
4. **Save** the changes of the tab.

## Known limits

- **A namesake is easily picked**: always check the publications of the proposed profile.
- **An INCOMING structure** is not yet confirmed by HAL.
- **HAL deposit requires at least one identified affiliation**: see [Deposit in HAL](/en/guides/hal/deposer-dans-hal/).
