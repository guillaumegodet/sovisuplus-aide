Keywords come from the sources, or from controlled vocabularies. A **Loose keyword** is not linked to any vocabulary.

## Steps

1. Open the **Keywords** tab.
2. **Select one or more controlled vocabulary**.
3. Type at least 3 characters and choose a term in the list.
4. To remove a keyword, use **Delete** on its label.

## What happens next

The correction is not immediate: the record stays locked while it is processed.

## Known limits

- **With no vocabulary selected**, the search offers nothing.
- **"No options"** may mean that the term is not in the chosen vocabularies: try another one.
- **The Concepts and collaborations chart** of the dashboard depends on these keywords.
