The dashboard sums up the output of the current perspective.

## What each block shows

- **The identity card**: the name of the perspective and, for a person, their IdHAL, ORCID and IdRef identifiers.
- **Publication per year and open access rate**: set the period with **From:** and **to:**. Bars separate **Open Access**,
  **Closed Access** and **Unknown access**.
- **Concepts and collaborations**: how themes change over time. Set **Max. occurrences / year** and **Font min ↔ max**, then
  click **Update**.
- **Worldwide collaborations**: the map of collaborations based on contributors' affiliations.

## Known limits

- **Publications without a date** do not count in the per-year chart.
- **Not all roles count**: see [Contributions and retained roles](/en/donnees/contributions-et-roles-retenus/).
- **A structure is the sum of its people**: see [Structures and perspectives](/en/donnees/structures-et-perspectives/).
- **The map depends on the affiliations provided** by the sources: it may be incomplete.
- **"Unknown access" does not mean "closed"**: see [Open access and HAL](/en/donnees/acces-ouvert-et-hal/).
