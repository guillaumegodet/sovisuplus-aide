The **perspective** is the person or structure whose data you are looking at. When you log in, that is you.

## Steps

1. Go to **Dashboard** or **Publications**: the search is inactive on other pages.
2. In **Search**, type a name. Results are grouped: **Researchers**, **Research units**, **Teams**, **Institutions**, **Other
   structures**.
3. Choose a result: the page is displayed for this perspective.
4. To return to your data, click **Back to my page**.

From **Structures**, the **Dashboard** button does the same for a structure.

## Known limits

- **"This perspective is unavailable"**: it does not exist or is not accessible. **Back to my dashboard** returns you to your data.
- **A hidden structure** does not appear in the search.
- **My Account**: changing perspective there requires a wider right.
- **Your rights do not follow**: viewing a perspective does not let you edit it.
