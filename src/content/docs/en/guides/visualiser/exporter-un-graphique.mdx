## Steps

| Chart | How to export it |
|---|---|
| **Publication per year and open access rate** | **Download into PNG format**, in the chart's tools |
| **Concepts and collaborations** | **Download the wordstream**, as PNG or SVG, once the chart is displayed |

Set the period and sliders before exporting: the image reflects the display.

For a list of structures, use **Export** in [Structures](/en/guides/structures/parcourir-les-structures/).

## Known limits

- **The Worldwide collaborations map** has no export button.
- **State the extraction date**: figures change with every data update.
