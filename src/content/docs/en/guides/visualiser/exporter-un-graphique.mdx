Two dashboard charts can be downloaded as images. The image reproduces what is displayed on screen: adjust the settings
first, then export.

## Publications per year

**Scholarly publication** card, **Publication per year and open access rate** chart.

1. **Choose the period** with the **From:** and **to:** lists, under the page title. By default, the chart covers the
   current year and the five previous years.
2. **Hide a series if needed**: clicking a legend entry, for instance "unknown", removes it from the chart and from the
   image. A second click restores it.
3. **Click the save icon**, at the top right of the chart. Its tooltip reads **Download into PNG format**.

The file is named `Publications_OA_<start>_<end>.png`, for instance `Publications_OA_2021_2026.png`.

## Concepts and collaborations

**Concepts and collaborations** card: the most frequent keywords and co-authors, year by year.

1. **Choose the period** with **From:** and **to:**: this chart follows the same years as the previous one.
2. **Adjust the display** under the chart: **Max. occurrences / year** sets the number of terms shown per year, and
   **Font min ↔ max** the size of the smallest and largest word. Then click **Update**: without this click, the chart,
   and therefore the image, keeps the previous settings.
3. **Click the download icon**, to the right of **Update** (tooltip **Download the wordstream**), then choose
   **Download as PNG** or **Download as SVG**. The icon stays greyed out until the chart is displayed.

The file is named `wordstream_<identifier>_<start>-<end>`, where the identifier is that of the person or structure
displayed. The text is black on a white background, even in dark mode.

| Format | Choose it for |
|---|---|
| **PNG** | A slide, an email, a web page: a ready-to-use image, at double resolution |
| **SVG** | A poster or a figure to edit: a vector image, sharp at any size |

## Good to know

- **The Worldwide collaborations map** cannot be downloaded.
- **Date the image** if you reuse it, for instance in its caption: figures change with every data update.
- **For a list of structures**, use **Export** in [Structures](/en/guides/structures/parcourir-les-structures/).
