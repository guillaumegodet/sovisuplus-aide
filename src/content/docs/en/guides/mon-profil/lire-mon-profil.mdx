Your profile shows what SoVisu+ knows about you. It determines the publications found and the structures you appear in:
check it on your first visit.

## What the page contains

**My identifiers**

- **IdRef**, **HAL** and **ORCID**, each with what the service says about you: **IdRef Information**, **HAL author**,
  **ORCID record**.
- **Copy** places an identifier on the clipboard.

**My affiliations**

- **Research unit**: your units, or "No related research unit".
- **My signature**: the form of your name and affiliation, to copy for your publications. With no data, the page says "No
  signature".

## Known limits

- **Viewing is not enough to correct.** See [Report a profile error](/en/guides/mon-profil/signaler-une-erreur-de-profil/).
- **The Scopus identifier is not displayed** here, even if SoVisu+ knows it.
- **Your profile does not show your rights**: see [Roles and rights](/en/donnees/roles-et-droits/).
- **Other accounts**: with a wider right, you can view another person's profile. The tab is then called **Profile**.
