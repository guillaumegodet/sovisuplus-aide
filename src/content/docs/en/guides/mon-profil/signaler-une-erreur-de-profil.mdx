Your name, your memberships and many of your identifiers come from your institution's directory. That is where they need to
be corrected.

## Steps

1. Note the wrong data in **See my account**.
2. Prepare the right value, with supporting evidence if possible: a link to your ORCID, IdRef or HAL record.
3. Write to your institution's [Support](/en/guides/support/), giving your name, your login identifier and your structure.

## What happens next

The correction made in the directory reaches SoVisu+ at the next update of the chain. After an identifier change, a new
collection may be needed.

## Known limits

- **Editing data directly in SoVisu+ is not always enough**: what comes from the directory is sent again at each of its
  updates.
- **An affiliation signed on a publication** is not corrected in the directory: see
  [Where to fix what](/en/guides/corriger/ou-corriger-quoi/).
