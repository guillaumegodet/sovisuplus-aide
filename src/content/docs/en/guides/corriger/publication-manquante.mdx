A publication you expected does not appear. Here are the causes, from the simplest to the least visible.

## Steps

### 1. Check the display

1. Check the **perspective**: the title must be **My publications**, or the name of the expected person or structure.
2. Stay on the **All my documents** tab and remove any column filters.
3. Search for a rare word from the title.

### 2. Look for a merge

The publication may be grouped in a record with a slightly different title. Also search for a co-author's name or the
journal.

### 3. Check your identifiers

Open **See my account**. Without the right identifier, the sources cannot find the publication. See
[Check my identifiers](/en/guides/mon-profil/verifier-identifiants/).

### 4. Check that the publication exists in a source

Look for it in HAL or OpenAlex: are you recognized as an author, with the right identifier? If the publication is in no
source, SoVisu+ cannot find it. Deposit it in HAL.

### 5. Restart the collection

If you have the right, use [Update all publications](/en/guides/publications/mettre-a-jour-les-publications/). Otherwise, go
through [Support](/en/guides/support/).

### 6. Check your role on the publication

A publication may be excluded because a contribution role is not retained by your institution, for example if you are only
thesis advisor. SoVisu+ does not flag this. See
[Contributions and retained roles](/en/donnees/contributions-et-roles-retenus/).

## What to report

If nothing explains the absence, write to [Support](/en/guides/support/) giving the DOI or link of the publication, and what
you have already checked.

## Known limits

- A very recent publication may not yet be known to the sources.
- A publication **without a date** can be in the list and not in the dashboard chart.
- For a structure, only the people attached to it count: see
  [Structures and perspectives](/en/donnees/structures-et-perspectives/).
