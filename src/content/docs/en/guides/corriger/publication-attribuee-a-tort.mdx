A publication in your list is not yours. It is most often a namesake, or a wrong identifier.

## Steps

1. In **Publications**, click the title to open the record.
2. In the **Sources** tab, see which sources cite this publication.
3. In the **Authors** tab, find the contributor attached to your name.
4. Determine the cause:

| What you see | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Many publications from another field | A **wrong identifier** on your record | [Report a profile error](/en/guides/mon-profil/signaler-une-erreur-de-profil/) |
| A single publication, in one source | An **error in the source**: namesake, mixed-up HAL profile | Have the source corrected; if you have the role, correct the record too |
| The contributor has the wrong profile in **Authors** | A **wrong identification** | With the role: [Manage authors](/en/guides/fiche-publication/gerer-les-auteurs/). Otherwise, [Support](/en/guides/support/) |

## What happens next

A correction made in SoVisu+ is not immediate: the record stays locked while it is processed.

## Known limits

- If the error remains in the source, a new collection may bring it back: correct the source too.
- Correcting an identifier does not necessarily remove publications already collected: report them to support.
