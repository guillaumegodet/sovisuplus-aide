Each institution updates SoVisu+ at its own pace: your instance may not be on the version described here yet.

## November 2026

**This help**

- Opening of the help centre, which describes SoVisu+ 1.6.4.

## SoVisu+ 1.6 (September and October 2026)

- **More tolerant search**, for typos and spelling variants.
- **Chart download**, for the Concepts and collaborations chart.
- **Edit account** available from the dashboard and the Structures page, for authorized people.
- **Deposit in HAL** from a publication record, if your institution has configured it.
- **An IdRef can no longer be deleted** by its holder: see [Identifiers](/en/donnees/identifiants/).

Features announced but not available yet are listed in [Upcoming features](/en/donnees/fonctions-a-venir/).
