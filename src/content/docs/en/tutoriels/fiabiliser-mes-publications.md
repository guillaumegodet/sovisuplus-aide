:::note[In brief]
**For:** a researcher discovering SoVisu+. **Time:** 20 to 40 minutes depending on the number of publications.
**Result:** checked identifiers and a publication list you can rely on.
:::

Without a role, you will follow the checking steps and report corrections to [Support](/en/guides/support/). With the
researcher roles on your record, you can make them yourself. See [Roles and rights](/en/donnees/roles-et-droits/).

## Step 1 — Check your identifiers

Open your name at the bottom of the side bar, then **See my account**. Check IdRef, HAL and ORCID: the sources use them to find
your publications.

Is an identifier missing, or not yours? That is the first correction to obtain, because the others depend on it. See
[Check my identifiers](/en/guides/mon-profil/verifier-identifiants/).

## Step 2 — Check your affiliations

On the same page, look at **My affiliations**. A missing or wrong unit is fixed in your institution's directory: see
[Report a profile error](/en/guides/mon-profil/signaler-une-erreur-de-profil/).

## Step 3 — Go through your publications

Open **Publications**. The title must be **My publications**, followed by your name. Sort by **Publication Date** and go
through the list, noting:

- what is **missing**: see [A publication is missing](/en/guides/corriger/publication-manquante/);
- what is **not yours**: see [A publication is wrongly attributed](/en/guides/corriger/publication-attribuee-a-tort/);
- what appears **twice**: step 5.

If many publications are missing or wrong, go back to step 1: the cause is often an identifier.

## Step 4 — Restart the collection

After correcting an identifier, click **Update all publications**. If the button is grayed out, ask
[Support](/en/guides/support/). See [Update all publications](/en/guides/publications/mettre-a-jour-les-publications/).

## Step 5 — Group duplicates

Compare the records carefully before confirming: a merge cannot be undone from the interface. See
[Merge duplicates](/en/guides/publications/fusionner-des-doublons/).

## Step 6 — Correct the records

Open a record by clicking its title, then correct what needs correcting:
[the information](/en/guides/fiche-publication/modifier-les-informations/) and
[the authors](/en/guides/fiche-publication/gerer-les-auteurs/). If the error comes from a source, also correct it at the source:
see [Where to fix what](/en/guides/corriger/ou-corriger-quoi/).

## Step 7 — Improve your presence in HAL

Open the **HAL incomplete filling** tab: it lists your publications with no HAL reference. Also look at the **Status in HAL**
badges: see [Read open access and HAL statuses](/en/guides/publications/lire-statuts-acces-ouvert-hal/).

## Step 8 — Check the result

Open **Dashboard**: does the number of publications per year look right? See
[Read the dashboard](/en/guides/visualiser/lire-le-tableau-de-bord/).

## Summary

- [ ] My IdRef, HAL and ORCID identifiers are correct
- [ ] My affiliations are correct
- [ ] I have noted what is missing, what is not mine and what is duplicated
- [ ] The collection was restarted after identifier corrections
- [ ] Duplicates are grouped
- [ ] Errors are corrected in SoVisu+ or reported
- [ ] The dashboard matches what I expect
