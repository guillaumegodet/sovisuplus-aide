:::note[In brief]
**For:** a person who monitors a laboratory's publications, often for a report.
**Time:** a few hours, in several sessions. **Result:** a reliable list of members and publications, and its known limits.
:::

Viewing requires no role. To correct, you need roles on the unit, given by your institution: see
[Roles and rights](/en/donnees/roles-et-droits/). Without them, prepare the list of corrections and send it to
[Support](/en/guides/support/).

## Step 1 — Open the structure

Open **Structures**, then find your laboratory in the **Tree** with **Search for a structure…**. See
[Browse structures](/en/guides/structures/parcourir-les-structures/).

## Step 2 — Check the members

Everything starts from this list: a laboratory's publications are those of its members. Under **Members**, compare with the list
you know, turning off **Currently present** to see former members.

A missing member or wrong date is fixed in the directory: see [Where to fix what](/en/guides/corriger/ou-corriger-quoi/).

## Step 3 — Spot missing identifiers

In the **Identifiers** column of the member list, spot people with no identifier: their publications are probably incomplete. See
[Identifiers](/en/donnees/identifiants/).

## Step 4 — Open the laboratory's publications

Click **Dashboard** in the structure's detail: it becomes the perspective. Then open **Publications**, and filter on the period
of interest with **Publication Date**.

## Step 5 — Group duplicates and correct

Sort by title to spot duplicates. See [Merge duplicates](/en/guides/publications/fusionner-des-doublons/) and
[Manage authors](/en/guides/fiche-publication/gerer-les-auteurs/).

:::caution
A member's publication enters the laboratory's list even if it was written elsewhere, before they arrived. This is not an error to
correct, but a rule: see [Structures and perspectives](/en/donnees/structures-et-perspectives/).
:::

## Step 6 — Take stock of HAL

The **HAL incomplete filling** tab lists publications with no HAL reference. The **Status in HAL** filter spots those outside the
laboratory's collection. See [Open access and HAL](/en/donnees/acces-ouvert-et-hal/).

## Step 7 — Read and export the report

On the **Dashboard**, set the period with **From:** and **to:**, then
[export the charts](/en/guides/visualiser/exporter-un-graphique/). Always state the extraction date and the scope rules if you
share these figures.

## Summary

- [ ] The member list is correct, or corrections have been sent
- [ ] Members with no identifier are spotted
- [ ] Duplicates for the period are grouped
- [ ] Publications with no HAL reference are spotted
- [ ] Figures are exported, with their date and limits
