## What it is

The **Research structures** page presents the structures sent by the directory, in three views: **List**, **Hierarchical
table** and **Tree**. Each structure has its **Supervising institutions**, **Members**, **Publications** and **Child
structures**.

Its **Dashboard** button opens it as a **perspective**: the dashboard and publications then cover this structure.

## Which publications for a structure?

SoVisu+ retains the publications of **the people attached to the structure** according to the directory.

| Perspective | People retained |
|---|---|
| **Researcher** | The person |
| **Research unit**, **Team** | The direct members of the structure |
| **Institution** | The members of the research units attached to the institution |
| **Other structure** | Its direct members and those of its child structures |

Attachment only goes down one level: for an institution, the members of a team only count if they are also members of an
attached unit.

## Indicators

A structure's publication indicators (publications, open access, HAL) cover the **last 24 months**. The **Dashboard** offers
an adjustable period with **From:** and **to:**.

## Filters

| Filter | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Include external institutions** | Adds institutions outside your scope |
| **Show hidden structures** | Reserved for people allowed to hide structures |
| **Currently present** | In a structure's detail, limits the list of members |

**Export** downloads the displayed list.

## Known limits

- **A structure is not a corpus of affiliations.** The scope starts from people, without checking the affiliation signed on
  the publication or the membership dates: hence the example above. Figures may therefore differ from a report based on
  affiliations.
- **A member missing from the directory removes their publications** from the structure.
- **A hidden structure** disappears from the list, the search and the indicators, along with the structures that depend
  only on it.
- **Display old structures** is not available yet: see [Upcoming features](/en/donnees/fonctions-a-venir/).

## How to report an error

A wrong structure or member is fixed in the directory: see [Where to fix what](/en/guides/corriger/ou-corriger-quoi/).
