## What it is

Each contributor of a publication has one or more **contribution roles**: author, editor, translator, thesis advisor, etc.
Each institution chooses the roles retained by SoVisu+. This is a frequent cause of gaps between what you expect and what
you see.

## How it works

Two institution settings apply, one after the other.

| Setting | Rule | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Publication list** | **All** contributors of the publication must have at least one role from the allowed list | A single contributor with a role outside the list excludes the publication |
| **Perspective** | **At least one** contributor belonging to the perspective must have a role from the allowed list | You appear only as thesis advisor, and this role is not retained: the thesis is not in your list |

If a setting is empty, it filters nothing.

### Fictional example

Your institution retains the roles *author*, *editor* and *translator* for the perspective. Camille Example supervised a
thesis without being its author: the thesis does not appear in Camille's perspective, even though Camille is among its
contributors. It does appear for the PhD student, recorded as author.

## Dashboard

The publications-per-year chart applies the perspective setting, and only counts **dated** publications. A publication
without a date can therefore be in the list and not in this chart.

## Known limits

- These settings are common to the whole institution: you cannot change them for your record.
- A role wrongly provided by a source can be enough to exclude a publication. On the record, the **Authors** tab may show
  "Default role - please check".
- SoVisu+ does not indicate that a publication was excluded by these settings.
